- `src-tauri/resources/dhamma.db`

- teacher names, biographies, titles, or descriptions

The database was supplied as project input. Its provenance and redistribution terms were not included with the source material. A distributor may omit the database and provide a documented local-import process if redistribution permission cannot be confirmed.
