Dhamma Echo

Privacy Policy

Dhamma Echo is designed to keep your personal listening activity on your device. It has no accounts, advertising, analytics, telemetry, or cloud sync.

Effective date: August 9, 2026

Overview

This policy explains how Dhamma Echo handles information when you use the desktop application. The developer does not operate an account system or a server that receives your personal library or listening activity.

Information stored on your device

Your favorites, listening history, resume positions, queue state, theme, playback speed, and volume are stored locally on your device. This information is used only to provide app features and is not sent to the developer. The bundled talk catalogue is opened read-only.

Information Dhamma Echo does not collect

Dhamma Echo does not collect your name, email address, account credentials, precise location, contacts, photos, advertising identifiers, analytics, diagnostics, crash reports, or telemetry. It does not use cookies or tracking technologies.

Audio streaming and third parties

When you choose to play a talk, Dhamma Echo requests the audio directly from dhammadownload.com or www.dhammadownload.com. As with an ordinary internet request, those independent servers may receive connection information such as your IP address, request time, requested audio resource, and browser or device networking information.

The developer does not receive that connection information through Dhamma Echo and does not control the audio hosts' retention or privacy practices. Audio is requested only when you initiate playback.

Data sharing and sale

We do not sell personal data or share it for advertising, marketing, profiling, or cross-context behavioral advertising. Dhamma Echo includes no advertising SDKs or analytics providers.

Data retention and deletion

Local app information remains on your device until you remove it. You can delete it by clearing the app's data or uninstalling Dhamma Echo. Because the developer does not receive this information, there is no developer-held account or personal library to delete.

Children's privacy

Dhamma Echo is not directed to children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app's no-account and local-storage design applies to every user.

Changes to this policy

If Dhamma Echo's data practices materially change, this page will be updated before those changes take effect where required. The effective date at the top will show the latest revision.

Contact

For questions about this privacy policy or Dhamma Echo's data practices, email builtbyamk@duck.com.
